About Inspire

Supporting people to live fuller, more independent lives.

Inspire Day Service provides a welcoming and supportive environment for adults with learning disabilities and autism.

Our focus is on helping every individual develop confidence, independence and practical skills while enjoying meaningful activities, friendships and new experiences.

What We Do

Skills for life. Activities to enjoy.

Our days combine practical learning, personal development and enjoyable social experiences.

Independent Living

Developing everyday skills that support greater confidence, independence and choice.

Life Skills

Learning practical skills through real activities, routines and experiences.

Learning & Development

Activities designed to develop communication, confidence and personal skills.

Social Activities

Fun activities, outings and shared experiences that help build friendships and belonging.

Meet Our Director

Lyndsey Curtis

The heart behind Inspire

Lyndsey founded Inspire in 2016 as a partnership after struggling to find suitable post-college services for her brother, who has Down syndrome.

She wanted somewhere that could offer life skills development, educational activities and, just as importantly, enjoyable social opportunities.

In April 2024, Lyndsey purchased Inspire Day Service in full from her business partner, becoming the sole director. Her passion for helping others and her plans for Inspire have continued to grow ever since.

“

I absolutely love my job. Seeing our clients progress in life skills, enjoy social activities and build lasting friendships, it’s like one big, supportive family.

Lyndsey Curtis, Director
